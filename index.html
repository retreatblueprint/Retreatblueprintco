SOPs your team can run without you

Revenue, expenses, and performance are scattered across spreadsheets and memory. Every decision is a guess dressed up as a feeling.

One system, the real picture

Finances, bookings, and performance tracked automatically in one place, so decisions are based on real numbers, not gut feel.

The approach

Hands-on, end-to-end, built to last

Whether on-site or remote, every engagement starts the same way: I get inside the business, learn how it actually operates, audit the gaps, and build the infrastructure that lets it grow without you being the bottleneck.

I audit before I build

Every business is different. I spend the first weeks understanding what exists, what's missing, and what's creating friction. All before touching anything.

I build systems your team can run

Booking pipelines, communication automations, guest journeys, SOPs. Everything I build is documented and handed off so it works without me.

I solve the problems you can't see

From custom lead generation tools to full sales mapping. I build what the business actually needs, not a templated package that fits no one perfectly.

I stay involved after the foundations are set

Fractional Retreat Operations Director (your part-time COO), ongoing ops support, or commission partnership. I structure the ongoing relationship around what creates the most value for your business.

Why retreats

I work in this industry because it has my soul

My name is Shila Kandiah. I have spent 10 years in operations management across healthcare, wellness and hospitality, helping businesses with process improvement, operations, and strategy. That mix matters in retreat work, where guest care, dietary and health intake, privacy and safety sit right next to bookings and budgets. I wanted to take everything I had built professionally and point it at something that actually mattered to me personally.

I have CPTSD and I am on my own healing journey of genuinely taking care of mind, body, and soul. Safe spaces that make room for wellness and healing are deeply important to me. I know what it means to find a place that holds you.

So right now I am taking my expertise toward my personal journey and partnering with retreats to help them grow, be seen, and serve the people who really need them. This is the work that has my whole heart.

When a retreat grows, more people heal. That is the business I want to help build.
