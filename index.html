My framework · The Retreat Blueprint

The guest experience is what people remember. The operation behind it is what makes it possible.

A retreat can be beautiful, deeply meaningful, and fully booked and still be a difficult business to run.

Behind every seamless stay are interconnected decisions. Pricing needs to support profitability. Teams need clarity. Booking systems need to work. Guest care needs to be consistent, without everything depending on the owner.

These aren't separate challenges. They're parts of the same business.

That's why I look at a retreat as a whole, connecting what guests experience with the operational and commercial foundations that support it.

The Retreat Blueprint brings that perspective into eight interconnected pillars.

The guest journey

From the first hello to long after they leave.

A guest's experience begins before they arrive and continues long after they've gone home.

It's shaped by the warmth of the first response, the confidence of knowing everything has been considered, the ease of arriving somewhere prepared, and the feeling of being genuinely looked after.

Behind those moments are thoughtful systems, clear communication, and a team that knows what needs to happen.

When the details work together, care becomes something guests can feel.

  1. 01 · Inquiry

    Heard quickly, and clearly.

    A reply process, answers to common questions, and follow-up until they book or decide.

  2. 02 · Before arrival

    Prepared and looked after.

    Forms, dietary and health intake, travel and pickup details, and a clear welcome email.

  3. 03 · Arrival

    Welcomed, not processed.

    A simple check-in, and a team that knows who is coming and what they need.

  4. 04 · The stay

    Held and at ease.

    Schedules, meals, housekeeping and support that run smoothly without the owner in every detail.

  5. 05 · Departure

    Complete.

    A gentle checkout, payments settled, and no loose ends.

  6. 06 · After the stay

    Remembered.

    A thank-you, a review request, an invitation to return, and a way to stay connected.

When these steps are designed together, the experience stops depending on one person's memory and energy.

Why wellness hospitality

I work in this industry because it has my soul

My background spans more than 10 years across healthcare, wellness, and hospitality, working behind the scenes to bring structure to complex operations, strengthen systems, and help businesses run more effectively.

But my connection to this industry is much more personal than my professional experience. Living with CPTSD has shaped a part of my own healing journey. I know what it means to search for spaces where you can feel safe enough to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and begin finding your way back to who you are.

Through that journey, I've come to understand just how meaningful the right environment, the right people, and the right experiences can be. It's given me a deep appreciation for the work retreat owners do, and the intention behind the spaces they create.

I also understand what it takes to bring those experiences to life. Behind the moments of connection and transformation are real businesses, with teams to support, decisions to make, systems to manage, and financial realities that can't be overlooked.

So right now I am taking my expertise toward my personal journey and partnering with retreats to help them grow, be seen, and serve the people who really need them. This is the work that has my whole heart.

When a retreat grows, more people heal. That is the business I want to help build.
