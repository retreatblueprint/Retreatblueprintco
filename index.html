The guest journey

From the first hello to long after they leave

A stay is felt long before check-in and long after checkout. I look at every step a guest takes, and at what happens behind the scenes so each one feels cared for.

  1. 01 · Inquiry

    Heard quickly, and clearly.

    A reply process, answers to common questions, and follow-up until they book or decide.

  2. 02 · Before arrival

    Prepared and looked after.

    Forms, dietary and health intake, travel and pickup details, and a clear welcome email.

  3. 03 · Arrival

    Welcomed, not processed.

    A simple check-in, and a team that knows who is coming and what they need.

  4. 04 · The stay

    Held and at ease.

    Schedules, meals, housekeeping and support that run smoothly without the owner in every detail.

  5. 05 · Departure

    Complete.

    A gentle checkout, payments settled, and no loose ends.

  6. 06 · After the stay

    Remembered.

    A thank-you, a review request, an invitation to return, and a way to stay connected.

When these steps are designed together, the experience stops depending on one person's memory and energy.

Why wellness hospitality

I work in this industry because it has my soul

My name is Shila Kandiah. I have spent 10 years in operations management across healthcare, wellness and hospitality, helping businesses with process improvement, operations, and strategy. I look at a venue the way a guest and an owner both experience it, because the two are never separate. I wanted to take everything I had built professionally and point it at something that actually mattered to me personally.

I have CPTSD and I am on my own healing journey of genuinely taking care of mind, body, and soul. Safe spaces that make room for wellness and healing are deeply important to me. I know what it means to find a place that holds you.

So right now I am taking my expertise toward my personal journey and partnering with retreats to help them grow, be seen, and serve the people who really need them. This is the work that has my whole heart.

When a retreat grows, more people heal. That is the business I want to help build.
