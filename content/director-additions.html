Who it's for

Retreat venue owners who want a partner in operations.

  1. More than one propertyOne operations lead across all your venues, with data-driven direction and a full summary of the numbers.
  2. Living offsiteSomeone to manage your venue operations day to day, and everything in it, while you are away.
  3. Focused on the experienceYou keep the fun, guest-facing side. I take the serious business: the numbers, the systems and the team.
  4. Every decision runs through youWhen the business waits on you, it can only grow as fast as you can. I help unblock it, so it can grow.
  5. Ready for a planPriorities, budget and forecast set with you, so the next offer or hire is a considered decision.

What a month looks like

A steady rhythm, agreed up front.

  1. Early in the monthNumbers review: revenue, ROI, EBITDA and guest retention, against your budget and forecast.
  2. First touchpointPriorities for the month, and the decisions that have been waiting on you.
  3. In betweenI lead the team, refine the SOPs, and keep messages, coordination and automations running efficiently.
  4. Second touchpointWhat moved, what is next, and the plan for the season ahead.

The hours depend on the level you choose, and are agreed before we start.
