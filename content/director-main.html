Who it's for

When the business needs another level of leadership.

Not every retreat needs a full-time operations director. But as a property develops, the decisions become more complex, and the need for experienced oversight becomes more important.

This partnership may be right for you if

  • Your property is operating, but performance isn't where it should be.
  • You have a capable team that needs clearer direction, accountability, or coordination.
  • Revenue is coming in, but profitability and cash flow need closer attention.
  • You're expanding, restructuring, or preparing for the next stage of growth.
  • You need someone to connect business priorities with day-to-day execution.

What I oversee

Connecting the decisions that shape your business.

Operational challenges rarely exist in isolation. Pricing decisions affect occupancy and revenue. Staffing decisions influence service delivery and costs. Weak systems create work that shouldn't need to happen.

My role is to look across these connections, establish priorities, and help turn decisions into operational improvements.

Business performanceReview revenue, operating costs, booking performance, and financial trends to identify risks and opportunities.
Operational systemsEvaluate and improve the processes, technology, and workflows that support the business.
Team and service deliveryStrengthen responsibilities, communication, operating standards, and accountability across teams.
Planning and implementationTranslate business priorities into actionable plans, coordinate progress, and evaluate results.

The numbers

Performance is more than occupancy.

A full calendar doesn't always translate into a healthy bottom line. Revenue, operating costs, booking mix, and cash flow reveal different parts of the business, and often point to different decisions.

I connect financial performance with the operational realities behind it, helping owners understand what is driving results and where changes can make the greatest difference.

Revenue performanceOccupancy · ADR · RevPAR · Booking mix
Operating profitabilityCost per occupied room · Break-even occupancy · Operating margin
Financial planningCash flow · Budget versus actuals · Seasonal forecasting

For exclusive-use or package-based retreats, the measures are shaped around your business model.

What a month looks like

From priorities to measurable progress.

Operational leadership is an ongoing process of evaluating performance, making decisions, and following through.

  1. 01ReviewAssess operating performance, financial indicators, team updates, and emerging challenges.
  2. 02PrioritizeIdentify the improvements that will have the greatest impact and agree on next steps.
  3. 03ImplementGuide agreed initiatives, coordinate with relevant team members, and address obstacles.
  4. 04EvaluateReview progress, measure outcomes where possible, and adjust priorities for the next cycle.

The rhythm, deliverables, and level of involvement are agreed upon at the start of the partnership.

Why fractional

The right level of leadership for your stage of business.

A full-time operations director can be the right investment for properties requiring continuous, on-site leadership. But not every retreat needs that level of permanent staffing.

A fractional arrangement gives you access to experienced operational direction with a scope and cost structure aligned to your property's needs. It's particularly valuable when the business needs stronger oversight, defined improvements, or additional leadership capacity without creating another full-time position.
