Questions

Before you book

How is pricing set?

Each level has a starting price: Essential from $1,800, Core from $2,800 and Embedded from $4,500 USD a month. The final fee depends on your property: its size, your team, booking volume and occupancy, and how much needs organizing at the start. We agree it after a free call, before anything begins.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Six months minimum. I recommend 12, so we work through a full season together, high and low.

Is it remote or on-site?

Remote for retreat properties anywhere in the world. Embedded includes on-site visits in Costa Rica, Panama, Nicaragua and Guatemala as part of a tailored scope. With Essential or Core, on-site visits can be arranged depending on what your venue needs.

Who handles the daily admin?

Your department leads carry out the work on site. For inboxes, booking updates, guest messages and payment tracking, Concierge Ops can be added to any level.

How does retreat coverage work?

Before we start, we agree which retreats I cover and which departments are involved. Around those retreats I coordinate readiness, team briefings and debriefs, and follow up on guest and organiser concerns. Between them, the regular check-ins of your level carry on.

What if my systems are a mess?

Most venues start with the Foundation Sprint to build the systems, then move to the fractional role to run and improve them.
