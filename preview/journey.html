The guest journey

From the first hello to long after they leave.

Every interaction shapes how guests experience a retreat, from the confidence of a first response to the lasting impression they take home.

01 · InquiryHeard quickly, and clearly.A reply process, answers to common questions, and follow-up until they book or decide.
02 · Before arrivalPrepared and looked after.Forms, dietary and health intake, travel and pickup details, and a clear welcome email.
03 · ArrivalWelcomed, not processed.A simple check-in, and a team that knows who is coming and what they need.
04 · The stayComfortable and cared for.Schedules, meals, housekeeping and support that run smoothly with a team equipped to deliver consistently.
05 · DepartureComplete.A gentle checkout, payments settled, and no loose ends.
06 · After the stayRemembered.A thank-you, a review request, an invitation to return, and a way to stay connected.
