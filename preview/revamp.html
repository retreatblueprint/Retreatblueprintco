I work in this industry because it has my soul

My background spans more than 10 years across healthcare, wellness, and hospitality, working behind the scenes to bring structure to complex operations, strengthen systems, and help businesses run more effectively.

But my connection to this industry is much more personal than my professional experience. Living with CPTSD has shaped a part of my own healing journey. I know what it means to search for spaces where you can feel safe enough to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and begin finding your way back to who you are.

I also understand what it takes to bring those experiences to life. Behind the moments of connection and transformation are real businesses, with teams to support, decisions to make, systems to manage, and financial realities that can't be overlooked.

This is where my professional experience and personal journey come together. My work is about helping these spaces thrive as businesses, so they can continue offering something meaningful to the world.
