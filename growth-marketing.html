Questions

Before you book

How is it priced?

Scope and price are agreed on a free call, based on what you need: strategy only, or strategy plus a dedicated team running it for you.

What's the difference between strategy only and fully managed?

Strategy only gives your team the positioning, channels and content direction to run with. Fully managed adds a dedicated marketing lead who creates the content, runs your social media and outreach, and builds your lead pipeline.

Do I need my systems in place first?

It helps. Marketing brings in more inquiries, so they need somewhere to land. If bookings still run through your inbox and memory, start with the Foundation Sprint or pair marketing with Concierge Ops.

Where do you work?

Remote for retreat properties anywhere in the world. On-site visits are available in Costa Rica, Panama, Nicaragua and Guatemala.
