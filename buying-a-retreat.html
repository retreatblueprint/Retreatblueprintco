Questions

Before you book

Does this replace a lawyer or building inspection?

No. I review how the business operates and flag anything your lawyer, inspector or accountant should look at.

How is this different from my accountant?

Your accountant checks the books, taxes and how the deal is structured. I look at whether the revenue keeps coming under a new owner: bookings, guests, team and systems. I work alongside your accountant and flag anything they should look at.

What if some records aren't available?

That's common with small venues. I work from what's available, such as booking platforms, reviews and a mystery-shop inquiry, and note anything to follow up before closing.

Selling a retreat property? Can you help?

Yes. A Listing Snapshot gives buyers an independent picture of how your business runs before they offer, so serious buyers move faster. It's priced per property.

I already bought. Is it too late?

Not at all. The 90-day plan is built for new owners, and it works best in your first season. Already past it? The Foundation Sprint sets up the systems your venue runs on.

Where do you work?

On-site reviews across Costa Rica, Panama, Nicaragua and Guatemala. Remote Desk Reviews are available for retreat properties anywhere in the world.

Can I compare two or three properties?

Yes. Many buyers start with Desk Reviews on a shortlist, then book an On-Site Review for the one they want to offer on.
