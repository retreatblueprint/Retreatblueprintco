Questions

Before you book

How much of your time do I get?

Two scheduled touchpoints a month, plus the operations work in between. Hours depend on what your retreat needs and the level you choose, and both are agreed before we start.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Six months minimum. I recommend 12, so we work through a full season together, high and low.

Is it remote or on-site?

Remote for retreat properties anywhere in the world. On-site time is available in Costa Rica, Panama, Nicaragua and Guatemala.

What if I don't have a team?

The Retreat Blueprint Concierge Ops team can handle the day-to-day under my direction.

What if my systems are a mess?

Most venues start with the Foundation Sprint to build the systems, then move to the fractional role to run and improve them.

Who makes the final decisions?

You do. You keep the vision and the big decisions, and you choose what else to hand over.
