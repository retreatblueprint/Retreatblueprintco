Questions

Before you book

How fast are guests answered?

Within 2 business hours, including while a retreat is in session. Interested guests also get a well-timed, polite follow-up in your voice, so they do not go quiet while you are hosting.

What decides the monthly price?

Your average bookings per month, which sets how much time the work takes, plus your team size and retreat capacity. Pricing starts at $250 USD a month and is agreed on a free call.

Who will my guests be talking to?

Your Concierge Ops team writes in your voice, using your policies and templates. I lead the team and I'm your point of contact, so you always know who is responsible.

Do I need to change my booking system or inbox?

No. The team works inside the inbox and booking system you already use. If something is slowing you down, I'll suggest a better setup, and the Foundation Sprint can build it.

How is this different from the Fractional Retreat Operations Director?

Concierge Ops does the day-to-day admin. The Fractional Retreat Operations Director leads the operations: priorities, numbers, team and systems. Many venues combine the two.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Three months, so the team has time to learn your voice, your guests and your policies, and you can see the difference it makes.

How are my accounts and guests' details protected?

You own every account, and nothing is moved or transferred. Each team member only gets the access their role needs, guest information is used only to look after your guests, and access is removed if you stop.

Where do you work?

Fully remote, so it works for retreat properties anywhere in the world.
