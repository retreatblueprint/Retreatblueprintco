Operations Director Readiness Checklist

1Does your retreat center need an operations director? Tick what is true

  • I lost count of the decisions my team asked me for last week.
  • New staff learn how things work by following me around.
  • To see how full next quarter is, I check more than one place and add it up myself.
  • I set rates and rosters from experience, without the profit per retreat in front of me.
  • A growth project has moved to "next month" more than once.
  • If a key team member left tomorrow, their tasks would not be covered.
What your total means

0 to 1: Holding together well. Write your first standard for the task your team asks about most.

2 to 3: The day-to-day is starting to depend on you. Start with the decisions audit.

4 to 6: An operations director would take real weight off you. Bring this sheet to a call.

530-day review

  • Week 1: weekly team meeting, and agree which decisions the team can make
  • Week 2: first standards written and shared with the team
  • Week 3: the numbers in one view and reviewed together
  • Week 4: review what changed and choose the next standard or project
