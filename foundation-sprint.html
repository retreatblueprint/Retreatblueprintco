Questions

Before you book

How long does it take?

A single piece can take days to a couple of weeks. The full build usually takes 1 to 3 months, depending on the scope and the size of your team. The timeline leaves room to hand everything over properly, with training, so your team can run it.

Are software subscriptions included?

No. Booking, payment and other tools are billed to you directly by each provider, so you own every account. I help you choose tools that fit your size and budget.

How is it priced?

Each piece has its own price, and you only pay for the pieces you pick. Use the quote builder above to see your total. Website optimization, the streamline guide and plan (priced on how many apps and tools you use), a fully automated financial tracking system and a larger custom operating system are confirmed on a free call, because the work depends on what you already have. Choose 3 or more pieces for 10% off, or the full package for 15% off.

Can you work with the tools I already use?

Yes. I audit before I build, so anything that already works stays, and only the gaps get built or replaced.

Is it remote or on-site?

Remote for retreat properties anywhere in the world. On-site visits are available in Costa Rica, Panama, Nicaragua and Guatemala.

What happens after the build?

Your team runs it from the SOPs and training. If you want ongoing support, the Fractional Retreat Operations Director or Concierge Ops picks up from there.
